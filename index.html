          Why it's a network effect, and why it's local

          • Same-side: every operator both posts jobs and picks them up. Each new crew in town makes the network more useful for every crew already in it.
          • Local: a new crew in Halifax doesn't help a crew in Moncton. The network has to be built town by town.
          • Starting from empty: launch in one area at a time (Moncton, Dieppe and Riverview first), with operators the founder already knows. The founder's own crew posts its overflow from day one, so there are jobs to claim before anyone else joins.

          About

          Built from a real season of window cleaning

          JobRoute started with Nate, a student who has run a small exterior cleaning business in New Brunswick since May 2025: 55+ jobs, over $12,000 in revenue and one part-time worker paid on commission.

          The spreadsheet problem

          Every sale and expense went into a spreadsheet. It recorded the business but didn't help run it: nothing on the phone, nothing for the worker, no way to see which jobs were worth it.

          Who it's for

          New and low-budget owner-operators in window cleaning, lawn care and exterior services, with one to five workers, who find big field-service software too expensive and too complicated.

          Where it's going

          First, use it daily in one real business. Next, test it with a small group of local operators and introduce a simple monthly subscription.
